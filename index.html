Consent is captured per contact, every send is gated through one centralized permission check, opt-outs are processed inbound, consent records age out on their statutory clocks, and a tamper-evident audit trail is written for review.

The CRM is the highest-risk surface in any platform, it both initiates outbound to consumer phone numbers and holds the relationship data that decides whether that outbound is lawful. The regulatory floor is rising, fast.

DNC and consent rules have shifted three times in the last year. Here's what changed, and how we already handle it.

April 11, 2025 · in effect
Consent can be revoked any reasonable way

Standard opt-out keywords (STOP, QUIT, REVOKE, OPT OUT, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, END) must be honored, and revocation processed within 10 business days, down from 30.

Our inbound keyword processor and centralized gate honor it in real time.
Jan 24, 2025 · vacated
FCC one-to-one consent rule struck down

The 11th Circuit vacated the rule the day before it took effect (Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC). Prior express written consent is still required.

We capture PEWC per contact regardless of how the rule lands.
Now → Jan 31, 2027
The 'revoke-all' deadline keeps moving

The FCC again extended its waiver of universal revocation, one opt-out silencing every business unit, to January 31, 2027.

When it lands, every outbound surface already routes through one consent check.

What we enforce, regime by regime.

RegimeWhat it restrictsWhat we do
FTC National DNC Registry
TSR §310.4(b)(1)(iii)
RestrictsOutbound telemarketing to numbers on the federal registry.
What we doA nightly scrub downloads and matches the registry; the gate blocks calls and texts to listed numbers unless an EBR or PEWC exists.
State DNC Registries
CO · FL · IN · LA · MA · MS · MO · OK · PA · TN · TX · WY
RestrictsSame as federal, narrower geography.
What we doSame scrub pipeline, tagged by registry type per state.
TCPA Calling Hours
TSR §310.4(c)
RestrictsTelemarketing before 8 AM or after 9 PM in the called party's local time.
What we doThe gate enforces the window using each contact's timezone; active-agreement, PEWC, and conversational-reply calls stay exempt.
TCPA Express Consent
PEWC
RestrictsAutomated calls to mobile numbers without Prior Express Written Consent.
What we doThe gate blocks AI Employee dials when there is no active PEWC on file for that contact.
TCPA Established Business Relationship
EBR
RestrictsDNC exemption for 18 months post-transaction or 3 months post-inquiry.
What we doEBR records carry statutory clocks; a daily job expires them and notifies the workspace owner.
CTIA Messaging Guidelines
STOP · HELP · opt-in
RestrictsOpt-out, help, and opt-in messaging flows.
What we doAn inbound webhook detects STOP/START keywords, sends a single CTIA confirmation, and ghosts the contact on STOP.
